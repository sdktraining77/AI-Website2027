# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Where is TechBridge Training Center located?
The center is located at 25 University Street, Amman, Jordan.

## 2. What courses are available?
The center offers Practical Artificial Intelligence, Full-Stack Web Development Fundamentals, and Data Analysis with Python and Power BI.

## 3. Do I need programming experience to join the AI course?
Yes. Basic Python programming knowledge is a prerequisite for the Practical Artificial Intelligence course.

## 4. Do I need programming experience to join the Web Development course?
No. The Web Development course is designed for beginners and does not require previous programming experience.

## 5. Do I need Python experience for the Data Analysis course?
No. Previous Python experience is not required. Basic spreadsheet knowledge is recommended.

## 6. How long is the AI course?
The Practical Artificial Intelligence course lasts 8 weeks, with 48 training hours.

## 7. How long is the Web Development course?
The Full-Stack Web Development Fundamentals course lasts 10 weeks, with 60 training hours.

## 8. How long is the Data Analysis course?
The Data Analysis with Python and Power BI course lasts 6 weeks, with 36 training hours.

## 9. Are the courses online?
No. Courses are delivered in person at the TechBridge training facility.

## 10. How can I register?
You can visit the center or email info@techbridge.example. Complete the registration form, provide the required information, and pay the course fee.

## 11. When is my place confirmed?
Your place is confirmed after the registration information is submitted and payment is completed.

## 12. Can I cancel my registration?
You can request a cancellation up to seven days before the course starts. Approved requests receive a full refund.

## 13. Does the center provide course materials?
Yes. Course materials are provided digitally.

## 14. How can I contact the center?
