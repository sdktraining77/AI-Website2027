# Registration Process

## How to Register
Applicants can register for a course by following these steps:

1. Visit the TechBridge Training Center or email info@techbridge.example.
2. Choose one of the available courses.
3. Complete the registration form with your full name, email address, phone number, and selected course.
4. Provide any information needed to confirm that you meet the course prerequisites.
5. Pay the course fee at the center.
6. Receive a confirmation email with the course schedule and start date.

## Registration Requirements
- Applicants must provide accurate contact information.
- Applicants should review the prerequisites before registering.
- Some courses may require a short placement discussion to check the learner's starting level.

## Payment
Payment is made in Jordanian dinars (JOD) at the training center. A place is confirmed after payment is completed.

## Cancellation Policy
Applicants may request a cancellation up to seven days before the course start date. Approved cancellations receive a full refund. Requests made fewer than seven days before the start date are reviewed by the administration.
