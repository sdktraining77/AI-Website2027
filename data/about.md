# About TechBridge Training Center

## Overview
TechBridge Training Center is a fictional training center that helps students and professionals develop practical technology skills. The center offers instructor-led courses, hands-on exercises, and small projects.

## Mission
Our mission is to make technology education accessible and practical, helping learners build skills they can use in academic projects and the workplace.

## Training Format
- Courses are delivered in person at the TechBridge training facility.
- Classes are held three days per week.
- Each class lasts two hours.
- Students complete practical exercises and a final course project.
- Course materials are provided digitally.

## Available Courses
1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
2. Web Development
3. Data Analysis
